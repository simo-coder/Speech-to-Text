Audio and Video to Text

Turn long recordings, meetings, lectures and videos into a transcript. Everything runs in your browser, so your file never leaves your device.

Long files are processed in 10-minute pieces, so memory stays low and the transcript fills in as it goes. Keep this tab open and your computer awake. Two hours of audio can take roughly 10 to 60 minutes depending on your device, and the first run downloads the audio tools and a speech model, which your browser then keeps. A word at the edge of a piece is occasionally clipped.
